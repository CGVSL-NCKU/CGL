We are Computer Graphics Laboratory (CGL) which is an interdisciplinary research laboratory. Our Lab's mission is to advance computer graphics research; its theme is the enhancement of human creativity and innovation through advances in computer graphics. 

This lab is founded by Prof. Tong-Yee Lee since 1997. The group in 2009 consists of 2 faculties, 8 PhD students and  10 MS students. Our lab was originally Computer Graphics Group at Visual System Lab (CGVSL) and renamed as Computer Graphics Laboratory on 2024/12/24.
